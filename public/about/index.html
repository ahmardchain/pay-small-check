Pay Small Check

About

Pay Small Check is for a buyer who is about to pay a vendor they found on X or Instagram. The agent buys that account's public profile, posts, a follower sample, and a complaint search from buy, then returns Safe, Caution, or Risky. Facebook is labeled coming soon and is not a check.

The data is paid for on Celo through the buy gateway at gateway.usebuy.ai. The buyer pays a small check fee on Celo. This is a reading of public posts, not a promise that a vendor will deliver.

This app is operated by the Pay Small Check project. It is not operated by Opera or MiniPay.
