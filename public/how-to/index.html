How to use

  1. Choose X or Instagram, then paste the vendor's handle. Facebook is listed as coming soon and cannot be selected.
  2. Choose USDT or cUSD.
  3. Confirm the payment in your wallet. In MiniPay the network fee comes out of that same stablecoin.
  4. Read the verdict and open the linked posts before you pay the vendor.

USDT can be a gasless signature when the wallet supports it. cUSD is a normal transfer. The hosted Celo payment service does not accept cUSD for gasless signatures.

A Safe verdict means the public record looked ordinary. It does not mean you should send money. A Risky verdict means you should stop.
