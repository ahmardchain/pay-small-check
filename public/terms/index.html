Pay Small Check

Terms

A check is a paid reading of public X posts bought through buy at the time you pay. It is not a credit report, a police check, or a guarantee that a person will deliver goods.

The fee is spent when the check is produced. If the data calls fail before any of them are paid, the fee is not taken and a transfer can be reused. A check that returns a partial record still costs the fee, and the receipt shows which calls were paid.

This app is operated by the Pay Small Check project, not by Opera or MiniPay.
